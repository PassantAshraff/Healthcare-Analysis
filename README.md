## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — 💰 Financial Overview
Summarizes the overall financial health of the healthcare center.

**Includes:**
- Total Revenue, Total Billing Amount, Total Cost KPI cards
- Revenue vs. Cost comparison visuals
- Profit/Loss breakdown by department or service type
- Insurance vs. out-of-pocket billing distribution
- Monthly financial summary

---

### Page 2 — 👨‍⚕️ Provider Insights
Analyzes the performance and efficiency of individual healthcare providers.

**Includes:**
- Revenue generated per provider
- Patient volume per provider
- Average billing per visit by provider
- Provider efficiency metrics (cost vs. revenue ratio)
- Top and bottom performing providers ranking

---

### Page 3 — 📈 Trend Analysis
Highlights important financial and operational trends over time.

**Includes:**
- Revenue and cost trends over time (monthly/quarterly/yearly)
- Patient visit volume trends
- Year-over-Year (YoY) growth indicators
- Seasonal patterns in admissions and billing
- Sparkline trend indicators in summary tables

---

### Page 4 — 🔍 Additional Insights
Deeper analytical views beyond the core financials.

**Includes:**
- Department-level performance breakdown
- Insurance claim analysis
- Patient demographics vs. billing patterns
- Service type profitability analysis
- Key outliers and anomalies flagged

## 🧮 Key KPIs Tracked

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Revenue** | Overall income generated by the healthcare center |
| **Total Billing Amount** | Sum of all patient bills issued |
| **Total Cost** | Operational and treatment costs incurred |
| **Net Profit / Loss** | Revenue minus total costs |
| **Avg Billing per Patient** | Average amount billed per patient visit |
| **Revenue per Provider** | Total revenue attributed to each healthcare provider |
| **Patient Volume** | Total number of patients seen in a given period |
| **Revenue Growth (YoY%)** | Year-over-year percentage change in revenue |
| **Cost-to-Revenue Ratio** | Efficiency metric: how much it costs to generate $1 of revenue |
